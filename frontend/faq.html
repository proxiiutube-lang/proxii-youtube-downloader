Frequently asked questions

A few helpful answers about using the downloader.

How do I download a video?

Start the backend, open Home, paste a YouTube video link, choose MP4 or MP3, and select Download.

Why can’t the page connect to the downloader?

The local backend must be running. In PowerShell, change to the project’s backend folder and run npm.cmd start. Keep that terminal open while using the site.

Why does a download fail even though the backend is running?

The backend also requires yt-dlp to be installed and available on your PATH. Some videos may be unavailable or restricted.

What are MP4 and MP3?

MP4 is for video. MP3 extracts audio only. The audio option may require FFmpeg to be installed for conversion.

Is it okay to download any video?

Only download content you own or are authorized to download, and follow the platform’s terms and applicable laws.
