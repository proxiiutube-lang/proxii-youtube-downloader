Bookmarklet

Open a YouTube video in Proxii without copying and pasting its link.

  1. Make sure the Proxii backend is running on this computer.
  2. Drag the button below to your browser’s bookmarks bar. If dragging is unavailable, right-click it and choose to bookmark the link.
  3. When viewing a YouTube video, select the saved bookmark. Proxii opens with that video link filled in.
▶ Open in Proxii

This bookmarklet sends the current page address to the Proxii site running locally on your computer. It does not start a download automatically; you choose the format and start it from the Home page.
